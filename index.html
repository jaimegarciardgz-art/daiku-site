Daiku

Create, organize and manage short-form video content.

In development

What is Daiku?

Daiku is a self-hosted application that runs on your own computer using Docker. It helps you review video clips, prepare short-form vertical videos and, only when you decide, send them to your own YouTube, TikTok and X accounts: by default, Spanish clips go to YouTube and TikTok and English clips go to X. Daiku is based on the open-source OpenShorts project (MIT License).

Daiku is not an online service: there is no Daiku account to sign up for, and your videos, settings and connected accounts stay on your computer.

Features

Clip preparation

Turn long videos into short vertical clips with subtitles, processed locally on your computer.

Review before anything is sent

Every video must be reviewed and approved individually, including confirming that you have the rights to reuse it.

Clips Radar

Find recent public Twitch clips through the official Twitch API and watch them with Twitch's own player. Daiku does not download them.

YouTube uploads

Connect your channel through Google's official authorization (OAuth) and upload approved videos with the YouTube Data API. Uploads are private by default.

TikTok drafts

Connect a TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit and send approved videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts. You finish and publish each draft yourself in the TikTok app. In development.

X (Twitter) for English clips

Connect your X account through X's official authorization (OAuth 2.0); the connection works. Posting videos to X is prepared but not enabled yet, and will only happen for videos you approve, within a spending limit you set.

You stay in control

Nothing is sent until you start or schedule it. You can disconnect an account in Daiku, or revoke its access on the platform, at any time.

Project status

Daiku is in development and testing. Uploads to YouTube are working and private by default. The TikTok integration only sends videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts; direct publishing to TikTok is not available. An X account can be connected, but real posts to X are disabled. Features may change as development continues.

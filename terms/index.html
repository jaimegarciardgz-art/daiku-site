1. Description of the service

Daiku is a self-hosted application, based on the open-source OpenShorts project, that runs on your own computer using Docker. It lets you prepare short-form videos from your clips, review and approve each video, find public Twitch clips to review (Clips Radar), and send approved videos to your own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

On YouTube, Daiku uploads videos with the YouTube Data API, private by default. On TikTok, Daiku only sends videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts; it does not publish directly. Daiku is in development and testing, and its features may change.
